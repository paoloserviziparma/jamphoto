Frequently asked questions

"But I never look good in photos"

Think of your passport photo: we can only do better. It's our job to know how to make you move and where to put the light.

Before shooting we always have a short briefing to understand what you want, look at a few examples and explain how it works. The goal is for you to look natural, on one of your best days.

I'm not twenty any more: is a portrait worth it?

Yes, and it's often exactly the right moment. A portrait isn't about looking younger, it's about looking like you: we work with light, posing and expression, not with retouching that changes your features.

What should I wear?

Rule number one: feel comfortable in what you wear. Brand-new or never-worn clothes make you stiff, and very busy outfits draw attention away from your face.

For a professional profile photo the frame stops at the shoulders, so trousers and shoes won't show. Bring a couple of outfits so we can try different looks.

In the studio or at my place?

We recommend the studio at Strada Martinella 76/1 in Parma, where we fully control light and background, and have plenty of natural light we sometimes use for special effects. We shoot tethered to a computer so you see the result immediately. And there's always a coffee, or a glass of wine if your nerves need it.

If you prefer, we come to your premises with all the equipment: all we need is a room with at least 4 × 4 m of free space. In that case extra time and travel are added.

How do I receive my photos?

We usually shoot 50–100 photos per look. We select 10–20, which you'll find within 24 hours in a password-protected web folder: share it with anyone you want help choosing from, and it stays online as long as you need. Where needed we do some light post-production.

How can I pay? And what if I need to cancel?

We accept cash, but prefer bank transfer or PayPal: 50% when booking and the balance at the end of the shoot.

If you let us know at least 48 hours in advance, we set a new date at no cost. If you don't want to reschedule, or tell us less than 48 hours before, the deposit is kept as compensation.

What if I come back for more photos?

If you're happy and come back later for another shoot, you get a 50% discount.
