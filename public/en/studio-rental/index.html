Frequently asked questions

What's included in the studio rental?

3 m wide backdrops in several colours, stools and wooden boxes, flash units with trigger, diffusers and umbrellas, projector, sound system, electricity (4.5 kW available), dressing room and bathroom with shower, access to the coffee area.

How do I get to the studio?

Direct entrance from Strada Martinella 76/1, 43124 Parma; the studio is reached by stairs. Parking is free.

Can you provide make-up, hair or catering?

Yes, on request: make-up artist, hairdresser and catering, including breakfast. Mention it in your request so we can organise everything in time.
