Legal basis for processing

The personal data described on this page are processed because this is necessary for the controller's legitimate interest in ensuring the proper functioning of the web services offered and in obtaining statistical information on visits and on the use of those services (Art. 6(1)(f) of the Regulation), and because it is necessary for the performance of a contract or of pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) of the Regulation) in order to reply to requests sent to us.

Types of data processed and purposes

Browsing data

The IT systems and software procedures used to operate this website acquire, in the course of their normal operation, certain personal data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet communication protocols.

This category includes the IP addresses or domain names of the computers and devices used by users, the URI/URL (Uniform Resource Identifier/Locator) addresses of the requested resources, the time of the request, the method used to submit the request to the server, the size of the file obtained in response, the numerical code indicating the status of the server's response (success, error, etc.), some information on the geolocation of the request and other parameters relating to the user's operating system and IT environment.

This information is not collected in order to be associated with identified data subjects, but by its very nature it could allow users to be identified.

These data, which are necessary to use the web services, are also processed in order to obtain statistical information on the use of the services (most visited pages, number of visitors by time slot or day, geographical areas of origin, etc.) and to check that the services offered are working correctly. Statistics are derived from server logs (Netlify Analytics) and do not use cookies or other tracking tools installed on the user's device.

These data (log files, traffic data) may be used to establish liability in the event of computer crimes against the website, and for the same reason may be retained by the ISP (Internet Service Provider) and by the web host.

Data provided by the user

The optional, explicit and voluntary sending of messages to the contact addresses shown on the website entails the acquisition of the sender's contact details (e-mail address), which are necessary to reply, as well as of any personal data included in the communication. This processing does not require consent, since it is necessary for the performance of a contract or of pre-contractual measures at the data subject's request in order to reply to the requests received; providing the data is therefore necessary to handle the request, and failure to provide them would make it impossible to follow up on it.

Sending messages does not entail automatic subscription to any promotional or commercial mailing list.

The data collected in this way are kept for the time strictly necessary to reply to the messages. If a business relationship is established, the data will be kept at least for the entire duration of that relationship.

Contact form

Filling in the form on the website in order to request information or book a session entails the acquisition of the sender's contact details (name, e-mail address and, optionally, telephone number), which are necessary to reply, as well as of any personal data included in the communication. This processing does not require consent, since it is necessary for the performance of a contract or of pre-contractual measures at the data subject's request in order to reply to the requests and business contacts received; providing the data is therefore necessary to handle the request, and failure to provide them would make it impossible to follow up on it.

Messages sent through the form are received and stored by the Netlify Forms service, which forwards them to the controller.

Filling in the form does not entail automatic subscription to any promotional or commercial mailing list.

The data collected in this way are kept for the time strictly necessary to reply to the messages. If a business relationship is established, the data will be kept at least for the entire duration of that relationship.

Recipients of the data

Browsing data are retained by the ISP (Internet Service Provider) and by the web host (the party that hosts the web server on its own physical machines), which keep them for the periods required by law.

Personal data collected through messages and contacts via the form are also processed by internal staff authorised by the Controller, acting on specific instructions regarding the purposes and methods of processing.

No profiling is carried out and the data collected are not subject to automated decision-making.

The website is hosted on the Netlify platform (Netlify, Inc.), which acts as data processor and guarantees personal data protection standards compliant with the GDPR. Servers may be located in different countries, both within the European Union and outside it (for example in the United States), and visitors' personal information may therefore also be stored in those countries. Transfers of data outside the European Union are carried out ensuring an adequate level of protection, on the basis of the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. Some links on the website open third-party services (for example Google Maps for the studio address, Instagram and Facebook for our profiles, or your phone or e-mail app to call or write to us). These services are contacted only when the user clicks on the link and process data according to their own privacy notices, over which the Controller has no control.

Rights of data subjects and right to lodge a complaint

Data subjects have the right to obtain from the Controller, where applicable, access to their personal data and the rectification or erasure of such data, or the restriction of processing concerning them, or to object to the processing (Articles 15 to 22 of the Regulation).

Data subjects who believe that the processing of their personal data carried out through this website infringes the Regulation have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (for example the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali), as provided for by Article 77 of the Regulation, or to take legal action (Article 79 of the Regulation).

Requests relating to the above articles must be sent to the Controller using the contact details given at the beginning of this notice.
